Engineered for Unbroken Workflows

Everything you need to turn your laptop into an autonomous workstation.

👁️

The Vigilant Eye

Sits quietly in your menu bar. Open eye with radiant glow signifies sleep-bypass is active; closed eye indicates normal system sleep.

🌡️

Thermal Overheating Guard

Monitors Apple Silicon & Intel thermal pressure via ProcessInfo. Automatically disengages if dangerous temperatures are reached in bags.

🔋

Smart Battery Floor

Configurable battery cutoff (default 15%). Never wake up to a drained MacBook if left unplugged on battery power.

⚡️

Zero-Prompt Instant Helper

Authorize once via Touch ID / admin password to install a scoped sudoers rule. Every subsequent toggle is 100% instant with zero dialogs.

⏱️

Session Timers

Keep your Mac running for 15m, 30m, 1h, 2h, or Indefinitely. Includes live countdown in your menu bar.

💻

Hacker-Friendly CLI

Includes the openlid binary. Integrate into automations, Raycast, Alfred, or terminal scripts with ease.

Scriptable Command-Line Tool

Manage your Mac's sleep behavior directly from your favorite terminal or scripts.

Terminal

$openlid on --duration 1h
