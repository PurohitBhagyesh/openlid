How OpenLid Works

Standard user-space tools sleep upon clamshell close. OpenLid intercepts kernel interrupts cleanly.

01 / INTERRUPT

The Clamshell Trap

Closing the lid fires a hardware interrupt directly into IOPMrootDomain. Normal utilities like caffeinate or NSProcessInfo.beginActivity are bypassed by macOS unless an external monitor is attached.

02 / KERNEL

Kernel Sleep Modulation

OpenLid controls the kernel flag via pmset -a disablesleep 1 paired with an active kIOPMAssertionTypePreventSystemSleep token. CPUs, network adapters, and background daemons keep running without powering the LCD backlight.

03 / SECURITY

Zero-Popup Touch ID Helper

Power management modifications require elevated privileges. OpenLid writes a scoped sudoers definition (/etc/sudoers.d/openlid) validated once via Touch ID, permitting instant toggles in <30ms without password dialogues.

Hardware Safety by Design

Running a laptop with a closed lid in a bag or enclosed space can lead to heat accumulation. OpenLid implements automated fail-safes to protect your silicon:

🌡️ Thermal Overheating Breaker

Monitors ProcessInfo.processInfo.thermalState in real-time. If thermal levels escalate to .serious or .critical, OpenLid drops all sleep assertions and lets the machine sleep safely.

🔋 Configurable Battery Floor

Avoid waking up to an empty battery. OpenLid tracks IOPSCopyPowerSourcesInfo and halts sleep prevention when battery drops below your chosen margin (default 15%).

🛡️ Signal Trap Cleanups

Intercepts SIGINT, SIGTERM, and unhandled exits to guarantee kernel sleep settings are cleanly restored to system default.
