Architecture

How OpenLid handles clamshell state without interfering with display sleep.

01

IOPMrootDomain & Win32 AwayMode

On macOS, OpenLid modulates pmset -a disablesleep 1 alongside an active kIOPMAssertionTypePreventSystemSleep token. On Windows, it engages ES_AWAYMODE_REQUIRED and sets LIDACTION 0.

02

Dual Mode: Taskbar or Window

Choose where OpenLid lives: discreetly in the macOS Menu Bar / Windows System Tray, as a standalone App Window in your Dock/Taskbar, or both simultaneously.

03

Vigilant Scanning Eye & Setup

An intuitive 3-step Welcome Onboarding flow with 3D animated vigilant eye (scanning left-to-right) walks you through instant passwordless Touch ID elevation and safety thresholds.

Hardware Safety

Automated fail-safes prevent battery drain and thermal accumulation.

Thermal Sentinel

Monitors ProcessInfo.processInfo.thermalState continuously. If thermal pressure reaches critical levels, OpenLid drops assertions immediately and permits normal sleep.

Battery Cutoff Floor

When running on battery, sleep prevention halts automatically if charge drops below 15%, preventing unexpected shutdowns while unplugged.

Signal Trap Teardown

Traps SIGINT, SIGTERM, and unhandled exits to guarantee power settings are restored to default state upon termination.

Command-Line Interface

Scriptable control for terminal workflows, shortcuts, and SSH sessions.

$ openlid on --duration 30m
Enabling OpenLid (duration: 30 minutes). Clamshell bypass active.
$ openlid status
Lid: Closed | Power: AC Connected (94%) | Mode: Both | State: Active
$ openlid mode app
OpenLid presentation mode updated to: Windowed App (Dock)
$ openlid off
Default macOS sleep behavior restored.
